* ABOUT ME
Over the past 5 years, I’ve developed expertise in DevOps/DevSecOps and am currently working as a Cyber Defence Engineer. I handle a range of alerts, from phishing and email, end user compute and IAM to cloud security reports from GuardDuty/SCC/Google Sec Ops.

I’m passionate about the software development pipeline and building secure software, utilising tools like Terraform for infrastructure as code and building automations in CI/CD. I’m always eager to explore new technologies, often using my home lab for hands-on learning.

A Linux enthusiast, I’ve recently been exploring NixOS/Nix-Darwin to manage my development environment declaratively. I am familiar with command line tools, navigating filesystems and shell scripting.

I've also taken part in a range of community events in the local Bristol area and spoke at the Atlassian Team yearly event in Las Vegas. Recently completed a CTF event for HackTheBox where our team came in top 300 of 6000 teams.
